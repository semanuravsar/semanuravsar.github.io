Research

My research interests span signal processing, wireless communications, and machine learning — particularly graph-based methods for neural signal analysis, channel coding theory, and reinforcement learning for adaptive wireless systems.

SIU 2022
IEEE
An Inequality for Query Age of Information and Age of Information
M. E. Ildiz, S. Avşar, E. Uysal
30th Signal Processing and Communications Applications Conference (SIU), May 2022

Established a formal inequality between Query Age of Information (QAoI) and Age of Information (AoI), motivating QAoI as a more informative freshness metric for real-time systems. Implemented and simulated AoI/QAoI minimization algorithms under i.i.d. and Markovian transmission delays across exponential, lognormal, and Pareto distributions.
